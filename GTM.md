## Exact target user

**Priya N., Head of Engineering / VP Engineering** at a **Series A–B SaaS company (40–120 employees)** with **8–25 engineers** actively expensing AI tools. She does not own procurement, but she *does* own the GitHub org, Cursor team invite, and the “why are we paying for Copilot *and* Cursor?” Slack thread. Her CFO just asked for a **line-item AI budget** before the next board deck.

## First 100 users in 30 days ($0 paid)

1. **HN Show HN (Day 3–5):** Title engineered for saves: “Show HN: StackSweep – audit Cursor/Copilot/Claude spend in 60s (shareable report).” Post Thursday 9am ET with a *real* example audit link showing $800/mo savings. Reply to every comment with math from `PRICING_DATA.md`.
2. **20 warm DMs to eng leaders** who *already* posted AI spend screenshots on X in the last 30 days. Message: “Saw your stack post — built a free auditor, want a brutal review?” Ask them to run it and share `/share/[id]` if they’re comfortable.
3. **3 guest spots in newsletters** (Engineering Leadership, TLDR DevOps) — pitch a *tool*, not Credex: “Free stack audit for your readers.”
4. **Ramp/Brex-adjacent communities:** Post in Slack groups for finance ops at startups: “Engineers bought 6 AI tools — here’s a 60s sanity check before renewal season.”
5. **Renewal-season wedge (May–June):** Many teams annualize Copilot/Cursor in Q2 — outreach to YC W24–S25 companies via **Bookface** “free tool” post (not ads).
6. **Internal Credex portfolio / waitlist** (if available): One email to existing credit customers: “Forward to your eng lead.”

**Week-1 traction if it works:** 2,500–4,000 landing UVs (HN spike), 600–900 audits completed (25–35% start rate), 180–270 emails captured (30% post-audit), 12–18 Credex consults booked (high-savings cohort ~15%), 4–6 share URLs posted publicly.

## Unfair distribution channel (Credex-only)

**Invoice-backed benchmarks.** Credex sees *actual* anonymized spend distributions across customers (Cursor Business vs Pro adoption, API burn percentiles). StackSweep’s public rules use list prices; Credex can later show: “Companies your size (47 FTE) median $38/dev/mo on AI — you’re at $71.” No pure-play competitor has **credit placement data** tied to the same funnel.
